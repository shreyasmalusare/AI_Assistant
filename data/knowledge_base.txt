## 1. Company Overview
JhaMobi Technologies is a premier IT Services and IT Consulting firm headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra. Founded in 2016, this privately held company of 11-50 employees specializes in Web Technology, Cloud Technology, AI Technology, HealthTech, and NABH Consultancy. With over a decade of expertise, JhaMobi helps hospitals navigate modern digital requirements, including the latest NABH and ABDM guidelines, to achieve Digital Maturity.

## 2. Web & Hospital Software Solutions
JhaMobi builds secure, custom software tailored to specific hospital needs. Core modules include:
- HIS & EMR: Systems for seamless hospital and electronic patient record management.
- LIMS: Advanced Laboratory Information Management Systems.
- Administrative Tools: Comprehensive HRMS/Payroll, Accounting, and Learning Management Systems (LMS).
- Patient Flow: Efficient Walk-In Management systems.
